NovaSprite is a next-generation AI platform designed specifically for game developers and indie studios who need **production-ready 2D game art, sprite sheets, and animations** with flawless consistency. 

Unlike generic image generation tools, NovaSprite is engineered from the ground up for the unique constraints of game engines.

---

## ✦ Why NovaSprite?

Generic AI models produce stunning concepts but fall short on game-ready implementation. NovaSprite bridges the gap with:

*   **Flawless Style Consistency:** Maintain exact art styles, palettes, and proportions across hundreds of character frames, tiles, and item icons.
*   **Perfect Grid & Frame Alignment:** Generate sprite sheets directly onto custom-defined grids (e.g., 16x16, 32x32, 64x64) without pixel drifting.
*   **True Transparency (Alpha Channel):** Get clean transparent backgrounds out-of-the-box—no tedious manually-rendered masking or chroma-keying.
*   **Engine-Ready Assets:** Pre-sliced environmental tilesets, combat cycles, and inventory icons optimized for immediate import.

---

## ✦ Core Pipeline Capabilities

### 1. RPG & Platformer Characters
*   **Isometric Movement Maps:** Multi-directional movement animation frames.
*   **Combat Cycle Rigging:** Perfect character frames with attack, hit, and death states.
*   **Chibi & Custom Archetypes:** Consistent proportion control across diverse art styles.

### 2. Smooth Frame Animations & VFX
*   **Elemental Spell VFX:** Multi-frame flame, frost, and energy blast sheets.
*   **Squish & Bounce Cycles:** Physics-accurate frame sequences for monsters, slimes, and particles.

### 3. Environmental Tilesets & Props
*   **Pre-sliced Top-down Tiles:** Seamless grass, stone, and forest assets.
*   **Stunning UI Inventory Props:** High-contrast, clean-edged weapons, shields, and items.

## ✦ Engine Compatibility & Integrations

NovaSprite exports assets directly in formats optimized for:
*   🕹️ **Unity Engine** (Spritestrips & meta files)
*   🤖 **Godot Engine** (Texture2D regions & import pipelines)
*   ⚡ **Unreal Engine 5** (Paper2D sprite & flipbook setups)
*   👾 **Custom C++ / WebGL** (Standard power-of-two texture Atlases)
